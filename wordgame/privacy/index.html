Famous Original Word Game — Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

In short: Famous Original Word Game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. Everything the game saves stays on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone:

  • Your best score, best level and longest word chain
  • Your wins and losses in Versus mode
  • Your sound and music settings

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can reset your stats from within the game, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Information collected by Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated information such as crash reports and general usage statistics. This information does not identify you and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version of the game adds a new feature, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
