Famous Original Word Game — Support

Help with the iPhone word chain game.

How do I play?

Type a word. Your next word has to start with the last letter of that one. Each word can only be used once in a chain, so keep it going as long as you can.

The game rejected a word I think is real. What should I do?

Email us the word and we'll take a look. Word lists are never perfect, and reports like this help improve the game.

I found a bug.

Sorry about that! Please email us a short description of what happened, along with your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About). A screenshot helps too.

I have an idea for the game.

We'd love to hear it. Suggestions for new modes and features are always welcome.

Contact us

Email leo@shmindle.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.
